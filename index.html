Read a scam like we do

A made-up listing with the five tells Trade Check looks for.

SXseller_8841 Joined 2 weeks agoE1

Charizard ex 199/165, mint. $180, price is firm.E2 Bank transfer onlyE3, message me on WhatsAppE4. 3 people already asking, first to pay gets it.E5

Likely scam · 88
  1. E1New account Fresh profiles with no sales are easy to throw away.
  2. E2Far under market A chase card at half its usual price is the bait.
  3. E3No buyer protection A bank transfer can't be reversed if the card never ships.
  4. E4Off-platform Moving to WhatsApp leaves the marketplace's refunds behind.
  5. E5Urgency Pressure to pay first stops you from checking.
